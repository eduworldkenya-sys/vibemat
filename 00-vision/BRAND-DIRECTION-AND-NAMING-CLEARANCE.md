# VibeMat Brand Direction and Naming Clearance

## Working brand direction

**Brand:** VibeMat  
**Tagline:** As We Move.  
**Product philosophy:** Everyone Moves.

## Meaning

"As We Move" expresses shared, ongoing movement. It is intended to include people travelling, people operating transport, mobility businesses, logistics providers, and communities that enable movement.

"Everyone Moves" is the organising product philosophy: VibeMat is a shared social mobility network with role-specific experiences, not a passenger-only app.

## Brand usage

- Use **VibeMat** as the working product and brand name.
- Use **As We Move.** as the working tagline.
- Use **Everyone Moves** to explain the product philosophy.
- Do not describe VibeMat as a transport operator or imply that it owns providers listed in the marketplace.
- Do not imply partnerships, integrations, availability, or legal exclusivity without evidence.

## Naming and legal status

**Status: NOT CLEARED.**

Preliminary web research surfaced unrelated businesses using VibeMat or similar names. The available research does not establish that these businesses operate in mobility, that their marks are registered in relevant classes, or that VibeMat is legally blocked. It also does not establish that the name is globally available.

Before major brand investment, company registration, or international launch:

1. Search Kenya's Business Registration Service for company/business-name conflicts.
2. Search Kenya's trademark register for identical and confusingly similar marks.
3. Search WIPO's Global Brand Database.
4. Search national trademark registers in intended launch markets.
5. Review relevant goods/services classes with qualified trademark counsel, including software, transport, logistics, marketplace and related services.
6. Check domains and social handles separately.
7. Obtain professional legal advice on confusing similarity, priority, territory, and intended services.

A domain being available does not confer trademark rights. A company-name registration does not necessarily confer trademark exclusivity. A search result is not a legal clearance opinion.

## Release rule

Until the checks above are completed and documented, treat **VibeMat — As We Move.** as a working brand direction only. Do not claim global uniqueness, trademark registration, or legal availability.
