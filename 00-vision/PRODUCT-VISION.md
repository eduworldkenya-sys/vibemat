# VibeMat Product Vision

## Brand direction

**Brand:** VibeMat  
**Working tagline:** As We Move.  
**Product philosophy:** Everyone Moves.

The tagline is a working brand direction, not a claim of trademark or domain clearance. Global and Kenyan name clearance remains open and must be completed before major brand investment or international launch.

## One-sentence definition

VibeMat is a social mobility network that connects the people, businesses, services, vehicles, journeys, and communities that participate in movement—helping them discover, experience, share, and connect through mobility.

## The problem space

People often need to make transport decisions without having a clear picture of what is happening around them or along a route. They may know a nearby stage, a usual route, or a familiar operator, but they do not necessarily know what other transport activity exists, what is moving, what is planned, or what useful connections may be available.

Transport information is fragmented across people, stages, operators, vehicles, routes, and informal communication. Much of it is time-sensitive and changes as journeys are planned, begin, fill, divert, complete, or get withdrawn.

## Product thesis

A person should be able to open VibeMat without first knowing what to search for and discover transport activity relevant to them.

The product borrows the *discovery principle* of a content feed: a user does not need to know exactly what they want before opening the app. VibeMat applies that principle to transport, with a map as the primary canvas rather than an entertainment feed.

## What VibeMat is

- A map-first transport discovery experience.
- A network connecting passengers and transport ecosystem participants.
- A place to discover declared journeys and other transport activity.
- An intelligence layer for route overlap, movement patterns, stage activity, and personal trends.
- A system that can surface activity locally while allowing exploration of other places.

## What VibeMat is not

- Not simply a taxi-hailing app.
- Not merely a matatu directory.
- Not only a vehicle-booking marketplace.
- Not a SACCO management system.
- Not a transport wallet or fare-payment intermediary.
- Not a video entertainment platform.

These distinctions do not prevent future transport services from being considered. They define the central product identity.

## Participants, not a single organising persona

VibeMat is designed for everyone who moves, moves something, operates mobility, or enables movement. Participants include commuters and travellers; drivers, conductors, and riders; bus, taxi, matatu, boda, and fleet operators; SACCOs; cargo and delivery businesses; travel and rental providers; and community contributors. A person may hold multiple roles. Role-specific experiences share one underlying mobility network. Ken remains a passenger use case, not the defining customer for the whole platform. He is a daily commuter in Babadogo, near Eastmar Stage, with Allsops and Drive-In also nearby. His usual destination includes Industrial Area and town, but his needs can vary.

The product should answer a simple question: **Why would Ken open VibeMat when he already knows the nearby stages and can walk to them?**

The answer must be useful discovery and intelligence he cannot reliably get merely by looking at the stage: current and declared activity, route and departure information, wider movement context, useful connections, road insights, and patterns—subject to data quality and freshness.

## Product principles

1. **Discover before searching:** useful activity can be surfaced without a search query.
2. **Map first:** the living map is the main app canvas, not a small dashboard widget.
3. **One network:** all transport participants contribute to one ecosystem.
4. **Activity over static listings:** dynamic journeys and transport activity are central.
5. **Visibility is not availability:** being discoverable does not mean accepting requests.
6. **Time and source matter:** planned, reported, live, and historical information must never be confused.
7. **Personal relevance without confinement:** personalisation can prioritise relevant activity but must not prevent exploration elsewhere.
8. **No fare custody:** VibeMat does not handle passenger transport money.
9. **Trust before certainty:** distinguish verified facts, operator declarations, community reports, estimates, and historical patterns.
10. **No premature building:** resolve product behaviour in notes before implementation.

## Scope sequence

**Agreed:** organise the product around Everyone Moves, with role-specific experiences sharing one mobility network.

**Open:** which participant groups and end-to-end workflows belong in the first pilot. Pilot sequencing must be selected by evidence, feasibility, safety, and network value—not by treating one persona as the permanent centre of the product.

## Everyday value across the mobility network

VibeMat must create useful value for different participants at the moments relevant to their roles. A passenger may discover options before leaving; a driver may publish an authorised journey; an operator may update service activity; a courier may communicate delivery coverage; and a contributor may share a privacy-safe road observation. Ken's 5:00 AM scenario remains one passenger test within the broader Everyone Moves model.

The value is not simply “show the nearest stage.” Ken already knows Eastmar, Allsops, and Drive-In. The value is combining information that is otherwise fragmented:
- What transport activity has been declared nearby?
- Which departures or journeys are planned, and for what time?
- Which providers are actually accepting requests, as distinct from merely being visible?
- What is reported about stage activity, route conditions, traffic, or weather, and how fresh is it?
- Are there relevant alternatives or route changes?
- What useful transport activity is happening elsewhere that he chooses to explore?

This is decision support, not a guarantee of transport availability or journey outcomes.

### Product value test

A feature belongs in the 5:00 AM experience only if it helps Ken understand, compare, plan, or act. A decorative marker with no useful, trustworthy information is not enough. Every time-sensitive claim must disclose its source and freshness.

### Habit hypothesis (not yet proven)

A useful morning briefing may give Ken a reason to open VibeMat before leaving. This is a hypothesis to validate with real passenger testing, not an established retention result.
